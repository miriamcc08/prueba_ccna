- **B.** Omitir validación y aplicar todo cambio a ciegas
- **C.** Sustituir transporte NETCONF por DNS
- **D.** Comprobar la edición solicitada sin aplicarla

test-only tests the configuration data without applying it, when supported. This is distinct from test-then-set and from assuming a successful validation is an applied configuration.
